# Janja Voice — modelo neural (janja-voice-v1.bin)

Rede própria (training/train.py), treinada do zero. Dados de treino, todos sob CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), usados para gerar misturas sintéticas:

- **LibriSpeech** (train-clean-100) — V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey, S. Khudanpur,
  "LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books", ICASSP 2015. https://www.openslr.org/12
- **MUSAN** — D. Snyder, G. Chen, D. Povey, "MUSAN: A Music, Speech, and Noise Corpus", arXiv:1510.08484, 2015.
  https://www.openslr.org/17
- **Multilingual LibriSpeech (Português)** — V. Pratap, Q. Xu, A. Sriram, G. Synnaeve, R. Collobert,
  "MLS: A Large-Scale Multilingual Dataset for Speech Research", Interspeech 2020. https://www.openslr.org/94

Nenhum áudio dos datasets é distribuído com o app; apenas os pesos treinados.
